# Incident timeline — proposed feature

An authorized staff workspace prepares an internal incident timeline from explicitly linked operational sources. Facts, missing evidence and unavailable draft summaries stay distinct. Source access is checked when opened. Existing legal decisions and deadlines remain under human control.

The proposed change prepares an internal follow-up task for staff to review and confirm. It does not execute operational actions, contact people or submit reports. Reminders remain inside the staff workspace. Generated incident summaries are unavailable; they require separate approval and implementation through the existing inference gates.

Status: proposed pull request, not deployed. Synthetic browser checks cover English, Portuguese and Spanish, desktop and mobile emulation. No live provider capability or real-device verification is claimed.
