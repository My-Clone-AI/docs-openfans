# Copyright draft

Provide only material you may share. Paste text or choose a plain UTF-8 `.txt` file up to 10 KB. The private draft expires after one hour. Keep the tab open while you prepare it; closing or reloading loses draft access.

When available, AI helps fill the draft. Otherwise, labelled text can fill ordinary fields and you can edit everything. Photos, screenshots, PDF and direct audio are currently unavailable: paste their text. The voice control explains the typed alternative; keyboard dictation depends on your device. Never dictate a signature.

Check the source, works and links. Choose one target, a notice route and your role. More links remain in your private source; the app never opens them or sends separate notices automatically. Fill any missing details, then confirm review. Changing details requires a new review.

Read and deliberately accept the complete legal statements, and supply your signature. No document statement or signature is carried forward. Send once. A received notice shows the service’s private receipt; keep it for status checks. If the receipt is unconfirmed, check the draft instead of sending again. Receipt means received for review, not proof of ownership, infringement or removal.

Use **Clear** to remove an unsubmitted draft. No account is required.
