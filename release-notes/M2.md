# M2 — ID check, creators, team and consent (development only)

- ID check with an ID document and a live selfie, done by an ID partner, with a step where you confirm
  the details it read. Built for two partners; **not available yet** (no partner contract), so adult
  content stays locked and the app says so.
- Appeals reviewed by a person; try again, withdraw your check; your account, devices and cancellations
  always stay available.
- Creator setup: profile drafts before verification, your own consent, public page with a real
  "Verified creator" badge.
- People in content: invite link, ID check and consent without an account, one-time withdrawal code,
  withdraw or report without buying anything.
- Recordings: age checked on the recording date; a person checks dates not captured in the app.
- Team helpers with their own login, limited rights and an end date.
- Consent text and retention periods are drafts pending legal review. Production is not live.
