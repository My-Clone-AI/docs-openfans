# M3 — vault, posts, human review and reporting (development only)

- Vault: direct private uploads that resume after a dropped connection, quarantine with a security
  check, prepared sizes without location or camera data, a safe preview, folders, tags, search and
  reuse. Each item shows its real state and the next step.
- Posts: Free / Members / Unlock, a preview of what fans see, and clear reasons when someone in the
  content is not cleared yet. Edits after approval make a new version for review.
- A person on our team reviews every post before anyone sees it.
- Scheduling in your time zone, re-checked at the time of posting.
- Reporting on every post and profile and at `/denunciar`, without an account; urgent reasons hold the
  post at once. Removal and consent withdrawal take the content down everywhere, including copies
  already sent.
- Fans get their own marked copies of photos; videos show a moving mark (a deterrent, not a guarantee).
- **Not available yet:** private storage on the public service, known-abuse image matching, payments.
  Production is not live.
